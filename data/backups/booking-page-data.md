# Guilty Pleasures Live Band Karaoke

### Watching a band is one thing... becoming their Lead Singer is another.

**Created and Produced by Joe Little** - former Production Manager for the Rock and Roll Fantasy Camp, professional musician, music producer, and audio engineer with over 20 years of experience.

Guilty Pleasures Live Band Karaoke is an interactive live music experience where the audience becomes the show.

Backed by seasoned professional musicians, guests step on stage, grab the mic, and perform with a full live band delivering powerful rhythm, lead guitar, and professional vocal harmonies.

No experience required - scan the QR code, just pick a song, and step into the spotlight.

A professional vocal host guides the night, supports singers, and performs alongside guests when needed so everyone feels confident on stage.

Live scrolling lyrics are provided so you never have to worry about forgetting the words again.

## Guitar Instructions (Guest Musician Page)
Sit in on second Guitar! A guitar, chord charts, and an amp are provided but feel free to use your own guitar. You don't need to know the songs fully, that's what we are here for, so just have fun and follow along with the chord chart. This is karaoke so the singers call the songs, be on your toes and be prepared to play the next song up! Plan to sit in for 1-3 songs depending on signups. It goes without saying.. be respectable and play the song as best you can. Don't solo over the entire song!!
